Wiring notes for this job

  • Every fixture wires in strict series — controller → fixture 1 → fixture 2 → ... No T‑splices or branches; data breaks downstream of any split.
  • Max 75 ft between adjacent fixtures on a chain.
  • A chain cannot bridge across two power supplies — each supply is its own independent run back to the controller.
  • Use SJTW from controller to the first fixture, LVLL flat 16/3 for buried fixture‑to‑fixture runs, and 18/3 CMX for any run routed through the building.
  • Downlights daisy-chain into the same 24V runs via water-tight M12 connectors (male in / female out) and draw up to 3.3 W each — they share the same per-chain wattage budget as the landscape fixtures, so confirm the budget before adding them onto an existing run.
  • Treat a chain at 95%+ of its wattage budget as overloaded — leave headroom for voltage drop variance.
